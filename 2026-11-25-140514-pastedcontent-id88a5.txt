● Both ways to add an ad now work. All 106 Ad module tests pass, including 9 new
  ones, and Pint is clean. I haven't committed anything.

  How it works, at /annonces/deposer:
  - Logged-in user: they're sent straight to the existing panel form (ad-panel,
    create page), so nothing changes for them.
  - Not logged in:
    a. They enter an email. It's matched against classified_ad_subscribers
       (ignoring case and spaces).
    b. If it's not there, they get an error and a link to the subscription page.
       If it is, the email is remembered for that browser session.
    c. They fill in a public form: title, content, category, end date, up to 5
       attachments.
    d. It goes through the same AI moderation as the panel. If the ad is
       refused, the reasons are shown and the uploaded files are deleted.
    e. Once published, the same ClassifiedAdProcessed event fires and the usual
       emails go out.

  A "Changer d'email" link resets the session. If the email is removed from the
  list mid-session, they can't publish any more.

  Files:
  - modules/Ad/src/Http/Controllers/PublicClassifiedAdController.php (new)
  - modules/Ad/routes/web.php (4 routes; email check and publishing are limited
    to 10 requests a minute)
  - modules/Ad/resources/views/public/classified-ad-create.blade.php (new, same
    style as the subscription page)
  - modules/Ad/tests/Feature/PublicClassifiedAd/PublicClassifiedAdRoutesTest.php
    (new)

  Decisions for you:
  1. Anyone who knows a subscriber's email can post as them. As you specified,
     nothing proves the person owns that email. A safer option would be to email
     a signed link and only open the form once it's clicked. The work for that
     is moderate. The 10-a-minute limit also slows anyone trying to guess which
     emails are subscribed.
  2. user_add is set to the subscriber's email for these ads. Editing permission
     compares it with a username, so guests can't edit their ads afterwards;
     admins still can.
  3. The public form uses a plain text box, not the rich editor. Its text is
     escaped and line breaks become <br> before saving, so guests can't inject
     HTML.

  Please run the full suite with php artisan test --compact.
